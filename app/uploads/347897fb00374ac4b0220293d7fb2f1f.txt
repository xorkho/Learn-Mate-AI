2. SERVICES OFFERED
=====================================================

Service: Hall of Fame Haircut
Description: A relaxing, full-service haircut experience for classic cuts, fades, or buzz cuts. Includes: consultation, hot face towel, scalp massage, hair wash, cut and style, and complimentary neck trims between cuts. Bookable online through each location's booking link.

Service: Hot Straight Shave
Description: Includes lubricating shave oil treatment, hot towels, moisturizing shave cream and hot lather shave, cool towels, and a post-shave cooling lotion treatment.

Service: Colour & Camouflage
Description: For clients wanting to change their look, covers grey hair or gives a full colour change, using long-lasting permanent colour applied to every hair.
